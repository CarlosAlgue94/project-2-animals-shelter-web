Events & activities

At Nous Colls, we believe caring for animals also means building community. We host nature gatherings, workshops, and open days to bring the shelter closer to people unhurriedly and with warmth.

Upcoming gatherings:

Solidarity forest walk

Date & time: Sunday, November 15 · 10:00 AM (Approx. duration: 2 hours)

Location: Nous Colls Natural Surroundings

An easy circular trail surrounded by nature, designed so our dogs can enjoy a refreshing outing alongside volunteers, families, and animal lovers. Before setting off, we hold a brief welcome to match each dog according to their pace and energy.

Free activity with prior registration. Comfortable walking shoes and water are recommended.

Count me in

Open house & responsible adoption day

Date & time: Saturday, November 28 · 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Location: Nous Colls Shelter

We open the shelter doors so you can see our daily care routine, visit our green surroundings, and spend peaceful time with the dogs and cats looking for a home. Our care team and volunteers will be on hand to answer any questions about responsible adoption and temporary fostering.

Free admission. All day long, you can stop by our information corner on volunteering and €1/month Teaming.

Book your visit

Family workshop: Understanding animal body language

Date & time: Sunday, December 13 · 11:30 AM (Approx. duration: 1 hour)

Location: Nous Colls Nature Classroom

A hands-on, interactive workshop led by canine and feline educators. We will learn how to read calming signals, recognize when an animal needs space, and build a bond rooted in mutual respect. Ideal for families with children or anyone considering adopting for the first time.

Limited spots to ensure a calm environment for the animals.

Past gatherings (Moments we shared):

Solidarity supply drive & afternoon snack at the shelter (October 2026)

Thanks to the generosity of over eighty neighbors and supporters, we filled our storage room with blankets, quality food, and toys for autumn. We wrapped up the afternoon sharing an outdoor snack surrounded by our most sociable furry friends.

Natural photography workshop to encourage adoption (September 2026)

Several volunteer photographers spent the morning in the woods capturing portraits of our animals in soft natural light with zero stress. Thanks to those tender photos, five of the participants have already found their forever families.

Community forest cleanup (July 2026)

Volunteers, donors, and friends of Nous Colls joined forces to care for and clear the forest trails surrounding the shelter, reaffirming our commitment to nature and all its inhabitants.
