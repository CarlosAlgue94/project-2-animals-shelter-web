Nous Colls Shelter

A home changes everything

At Nous Colls, we believe affection is shown through action. We are a nature-surrounded shelter where abandoned or vulnerable dogs and cats find peace, receive the care they need, and wait for the chance to become part of a loving family. Without rushing and with deep warmth, we work every day to build genuine bonds between people and animals.

What moves us

Mission: To offer a second chance to abandoned or vulnerable animals through dedicated care, well-being, and responsible adoption. We work every day so that every dog and cat regains trust and finds a loving forever family.

Values: Deep respect for animals and the nature around us, the warmth of a real home, responsibility, and affectionate closeness in every gesture and every adoption.

Vision: To shift society’s perspective toward a world where every animal is recognized for what they truly are: a living being with feelings, the right to be loved, and respected as a full member of the family.
