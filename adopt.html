Meet your new life companion

Every one of our furry friends has their own story, pace, and personality. Take your time getting to know them—the gaze you are looking for might be waiting right here.

How does adoption work at Nous Colls?

A simple and responsible process

  1. Tell us about your lifestyle: Fill out a short form so we can understand your daily routine and guide you toward the companion who fits you best.
  2. Meet with no rush: Visit our nature-surrounded shelter to spend time together, walk or play, and see if a genuine bond sparks.
  3. Welcome home: We finalize the adoption with all veterinary care up to date and support you through the first days of settling in.
