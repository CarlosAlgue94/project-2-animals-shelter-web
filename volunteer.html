Become a volunteer

Hands, time, and affection are what keep Nous Colls alive. Whether you can come once a week or help out at occasional events, your presence makes a real difference in our animals' daily well-being.

How can you help at the shelter?

  • Forest walks & socialization — Accompany our dogs on calm walks through the surrounding woods, helping them exercise, build trust, and enjoy human company.
  • Daily care & shelter upkeep — Help keep kennels, cat areas, and green spaces clean, fresh, and comfortable so every furry friend feels safe.
  • Events, photography & outreach — Support us during open days, take natural portraits for adoption profiles, or help spread the word on social media.

Join our volunteer team

Fill out our volunteer form, and we'll get in touch to discuss your availability, interests, and the best way for you to contribute. We welcome all levels of experience and commitment.
